About the academy

More than practice. Build your game.

Saurabh Chavan Badminton Academy helps players develop stronger fundamentals, smarter movement and confidence in match situations. Training focuses on consistent improvement, discipline and the habits that support long-term progress.

Whether you are building your foundation or working toward competitive play, the goal is to help you train with intent and keep getting better.

  • Racket skills and shot control
  • Footwork and court movement
  • Fitness and agility development
  • Rally and match practice
  • Competitive match awareness
  • Tournament preparation focus
Training programs

Make every session count.

Build a stronger foundation with practice that develops technique, movement, consistency and competitive confidence.

Skills & technique

Footwork & fitness

Improve court coverage, balance, agility, reaction, stamina and recovery movement.

Rallies & match play

Tournament preparation

Focused preparation for players aiming to compete at district, state and national levels.
