About the academy

More than practice. Build your game.

Saurabh Chavan Badminton Academy focuses on developing stronger fundamentals, smarter movement and confidence in match situations. Training is centered on consistent improvement, discipline and long-term progress.

Whether you are learning the basics or working toward competitive play, contact the coach to discuss your goals and the right batch.

  • Racket skills and shot control
  • Footwork and court movement
  • Fitness and agility
  • Rally and match practice
  • Match awareness
  • Tournament preparation focus
